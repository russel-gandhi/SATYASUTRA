## Goal

The live probe showed Wikidata answering 403 to a generic User-Agent. Wikimedia services expect a descriptive User-Agent with
real contact details. Make the User-Agent configurable so the team can supply a real contact, without writing any personal
address into the repository.

## Done when

- `runtime.yaml` takes a User-Agent template and the contact is read from an environment variable (`FORGE_CONTACT`), never committed.
- With `FORGE_CONTACT` set by the team, `python scripts/probe_sources.py` shows `wikidata` as `reachable`, and the result is recorded in `docs/probe_results.md`.
- Without it, the probe still runs and says why Wikidata is blocked.

## Notes

**Blocked on the team:** needs a real contact (an email or project URL the team is willing to send to Wikimedia). Do not use anyone's personal address without their say-so.
