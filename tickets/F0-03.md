## Goal

The shared types every lane codes against: Post, Claim, Evidence, Verdict, CorrectionCard, DetectorResult, VariantEdge, LedgerEntry.
Validators enforce the rules, not conventions.

## Done when

- `pytest tests/unit/test_schemas.py` passes, including: empty evidence forces `UNVERIFIABLE_YET` (claim) or `INCONCLUSIVE` (media); `SUPPORTED` or `REFUTED` without a Tier 1 or 2 record is coerced; a claim-axis verdict cannot carry a media label; a failed-gate detector result cannot carry a score.
