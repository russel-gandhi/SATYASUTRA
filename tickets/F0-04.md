## Goal

Append-only, hash-chained, idempotent ledger behind a `LedgerStore` interface (SQLite now, Postgres later), plus `scripts/verify_ledger.py`.

## Done when

- `pytest tests/unit/test_ledger.py` passes: chain verifies; UPDATE and DELETE are refused; a repeated idempotency key returns the existing entry; editing a stored payload (with the guard removed) makes verification fail at the right sequence number.
- `python scripts/verify_ledger.py --db <path>` exits 0 on a good ledger and 1 on a tampered one.

## Notes

`hash = SHA-256(prev_hash || seq || kind || payload_sha256 || code_version)`; payload is canonical sorted-key JSON.
