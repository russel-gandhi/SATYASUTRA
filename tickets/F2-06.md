## Goal

"High-throughput" becomes a measured claim. Targets live in a config file, a script measures them, and the pitch quotes the measured value next to the target.

## Done when

- `config/slo.yaml` holds the Part 3.1 targets that apply to Phase 2: sustained intake (starting target 600 posts per minute on one worker), fast-clock latency per post (p95 under 200 ms, including fingerprinting when media is on), cache hit (p95 under 1 s) and escalation share (at most 5%). Each is marked a **starting target, not a result**. Targets for the agent, image and audio paths are not added until those phases measure them.
- `scripts/bench_pipeline.py` replays **real posts captured earlier from the live stream** (the capture is written by the script's own `capture` command, kept out of git, and its date and size are printed in the result) through the fast clock at full speed, and separately times a live window. Replaying captured real posts is allowed for a benchmark; it is not a demo and never feeds a verdict.
- The script writes a table (target, measured, pass or miss, sample size, date, machine) to `docs/benchmarks.md` and exits non-zero if the file cannot be written. A missed target is reported as a miss, not tuned away.
- It reports the single biggest cost in the per-post path (normalise, fingerprint, cluster, cache) so the next optimisation is chosen from data.
- `tests/unit/test_slo_config.py` checks the file parses, every target has a unit and a direction (at most or at least), and the benchmark's verdict logic marks a pass and a miss correctly on numbers the test supplies.

## Notes

Say what the number is: one Windows laptop, Python 3.14, SQLite, no GPU. Do not extrapolate to a production deployment.
