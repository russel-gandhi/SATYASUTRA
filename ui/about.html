How it works

You send something you doubt. We go and look, then tell you what we found and how sure we are. We would rather say "we cannot tell" than guess.

The path of one check

1. You sendtext, a link, a photo, a video, a voice note or a PDF, in English, Hindi or Kannada
2. We readclaims are pulled out and translated to English; voice is transcribed; pages and PDFs are read as text
3. We lookfact-check archives, official RBI and SEBI releases, and news from outlets we list
4. Rules decideplain code, not a model, turns the evidence into a verdict
5. You get a reportverdict, sources, graph and limits, with a link you can share

What the verdicts mean

  • SUPPORTED: at least two reputable outlets report it as fact, or a fact-checker rated it true.
  • FALSE: a fact-checker or reputable outlets say it is false, or an official number contradicts it.
  • MISLEADING: true in part but missing context.
  • UNVERIFIED: we found evidence but not enough to decide.
  • INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: we found nothing. That is not proof it is false.

What we will not do

  • Say "genuine" about a picture or voice. A clean result only means our detectors saw nothing.
  • Show a source we did not actually retrieve, or call a claim false because we found nothing.
  • File anything with a government portal for you, get past a login or a CAPTCHA, or pretend to be a browser to get around a block.
  • Pretend to watch the whole country. Dashboards on the investigator side use only what was checked on this server.

Known limits (measured or observed)

  • The edit detector missed about 4 in 10 spliced photos after sharing; the AI-image detector missed about 1 in 10. Neither finds face swaps.
  • The voice detector was tested on a small set of machine voices, not on cloned real people.
  • Voice notes in Indian languages can be mis-heard; we show the transcript so you can check it.
  • News outlets copy each other, so news-only support is capped at medium confidence.
  • The Hindi wording on reports and cards has not been reviewed by a native speaker.
  • PIB, the health ministry and the cybercrime portal refuse our automated requests, so they are not used.
