Before you start
  • Put the call on speaker and keep this page open.
  • This page listens through your phone's microphone only while the button is on, in short pieces.
  • Nothing is recorded or kept. The sound is turned into words, checked, and thrown away.
  • The other person is not told. We cannot say whether listening to a call this way is allowed where you are. Your choice.
  • It can miss scams and it can be wrong. It will never tell you a call is safe.
